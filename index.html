Controls and symbols
  • Mouse/touch: drag back from the dish, release to launch.
  • Keyboard: ←→ aim angle, ↑↓ power, Enter launch (or start, next level, retry), Space tablecloth tug, R restart, M mute. Hold Shift for finer aim steps.
  • Aiming: angle 0 points right and positive angles point down the screen. Esc cancels a mouse or touch drag.
  • Cranberry spill: diagonal stripes with a ❄ marker. It is slippery, so dishes slide farther.
  • Mashed potato: dotted fill with a ● marker. It is sticky, so dishes get trapped.
  • Hazards: hatched outlines with a ⚠ marker.
